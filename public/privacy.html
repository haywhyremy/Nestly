Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Nestly is a baby tracking app that stores your data securely. Your data is stored in the EU (London region) and is never sold to third parties.

We collect only the minimum data needed to provide the service: your email address, baby's name and date of birth, and feeding/nappy/sleep logs.

You can export all your data as CSV or delete your account at any time from the Settings screen.

For questions, contact us at support@nestly.app.
